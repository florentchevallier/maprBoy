Wahoondrer custom Wahoo maps with Wandrer & Squadrats

1Folders

Easiest: press Set up my folders and choose where they should live. The page creates the folders there, plus one for the OpenStreetMap map data; then drop your Wandrer and Squadrats files into them (here below, or in your file manager with "Open"). Choices are remembered.

Output: the maps are written to wmc_classique and wmc_wandrer inside it (rebuilt at each run: use a dedicated folder). Work files: temporary files made while building; you can ignore this folder. It holds the merged Wandrer data (wandrer.osm) handed to WMC.

2Wandrer exports

Ticked exports are merged; where they overlap, the most recent one wins. "Near" is the town where you ride most in that export.

Which exports to use:

Wandrer gives three kinds of export: bike, foot and combined. The map is made from one kind. Pro: “all types” mixes them; “latest” follows the type of your newest export.

Drop your Wandrer .kmz files here (or click to choose them)

3Tiles

Wahoo maps are made of zoom-8 tiles (about 100 × 70 km). Click the map to add or remove a tile. Orange = to build, coloured rectangles = area covered by an export, one colour per type (hover a row above to find it).

4Wandrer layer

Roads farther than this from a road you rode are left out to keep the tiles light. In an area where you have ridden nothing yet, no unvisited road is shown: choose a larger distance or "everywhere" (heavier tiles). Wandrer only counts roads open to the public, so the orange layer shows which ones count.

6Map data from OpenStreetMap

The base maps are built from OpenStreetMap extracts (Geofabrik regions) kept on your computer. WMC downloads missing regions by itself and renews those older than 14 days; here you can see them and update or add some yourself. Some are big (Austria ≈ 0.8 GB).
