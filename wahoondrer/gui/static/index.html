Basemap & POIs from OpenStreetMap

Base map

OpenStreetMap data: . Missing or old regions are downloaded when you make the maps.

The base maps are built from OpenStreetMap extracts (Geofabrik regions) kept on your computer. WMC downloads missing regions by itself and renews those older than 14 days; here you can see them and update or add some yourself. Some are big (Austria ≈ 0.8 GB).

Points of interest

Choose which places are drawn on the map and from which zoom level. The default style is kept: unticking or changing a line never touches it, and “Back to the defaults” restores it.

Add your own type of place, by its OpenStreetMap tag (find tags on taginfo, check the data on overpass-turbo).

After a change, the tiles must be rebuilt and the theme copied again to your device (“Make the maps” does both). More places and lower zoom levels make the tiles heavier and the map busier: add them with care.

Wandrer

Exports

Drop your Wandrer .kmz files here (or click to choose them): they are copied into your Wandrer folder, named by date, town and type, never replacing a file

Only the exports of the chosen type are listed (the type is read inside each file); ticked ones are merged, the most recent wins where they overlap, and are shown on the map. The choice is kept for each device.

Roads

Roads farther than this from a road you rode are left out to keep the tiles light. In an area where you have ridden nothing yet, no unvisited road is shown: choose a larger distance or "everywhere" (heavier tiles). Wandrer only counts roads open to the public, so the orange layer shows which ones count.

Folders and settings



  
Easiest: press Set up my folders and choose where they should live. The page creates the folders there, plus one for the OpenStreetMap map data; then drop your Wandrer and Squadrats files into them (here below, or in your file manager with "Open"). Choices are remembered.

  

  
  
  
  
  
Output: the maps are written to wmc_classic and wahoondrer_maps inside it (one tile per coordinates: a new run replaces only the tiles it rebuilds and keeps the others; to keep several versions of a tile, copy it elsewhere yourself). Work files: temporary files made while building; you can ignore this folder. It holds the merged Wandrer data (wandrer.osm) handed to WMC.
